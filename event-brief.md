# Event brief: UGREEN Hackathon 2026 on AI NAS (Devpost)

Source: https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/ and /rules, read 2026-11-12. Starter kit: https://ai.ugreen.com/pages/hackathon-starter-kit (uliya-skill-kit 0.7.3-en, catalog 2.0.0).

## Dates (Pacific Time)
- Round 1 submission closes **Sun 2026-11-15 23:45 PT** (Mon 02:45 GMT-5). Registration closes at the same time.
- Round 1 judging Nov 16–19. Up to 30 finalists invited Nov 21 00:00 PT by email.
- Finalists: one assigned 24 h day of remote access to a UGREEN AI NAS iDX6011 Pro (64 GB) between Nov 22 and Nov 28.
- Round 2 submission closes Mon 2026-11-30 23:45 PT. Judging Dec 1–5. Winners ~Dec 6.

## Round 1 deliverables (no NAS, repo or video required)
1. **Project Proposal PDF**, ≤1 MB, ≤500 English words over ten answers (team consent, names, signatures and third-party license info excluded from the count):
   1. What does your Skill do? (one sentence)
   2. Who is the target user, and what is their problem?
   3. What is your solution?
   4. What is the main user flow?
   5. How is it different from existing tools or common AI solutions?
   6. Which UGREEN AI NAS features does it use, and at which step?
   7. Technical approach: planned workflow, supported NAS CLI capabilities, expert agent orchestration, required inputs, permissions, AI model choices, data handling.
   8. What is built, what is not, what still needs NAS testing?
   9. Finalist validation plan: two core test cases and one edge case.
   10. Known limits, risks, outside dependencies.
   Third-party components with sources and licenses must be identified.
2. **Pre-test Skill Package ZIP** per the Uliya Skill Development Standard: SKILL.md (frontmatter name 2–64 chars lowercase-hyphen, description ≤1,024 chars; user goal, required inputs, supported CLI capabilities, expert orchestration, completion/failure handling); optional references/*.md each linked directly from SKILL.md. ZIP and unzipped ≤10 MB, ≤1 MB per file, ≤100 files. No manifests, scripts, executables, binaries. Disclose third-party components, principal functionality developed for the hackathon, and AI tools used. Need not be installable yet.

## Judging (Rounds 1 and 2, equally weighted)
- **User Value**: clear user problem, clear workflow, useful feedback, meaningful result.
- **Innovation & Differentiation**: substantive new function, interaction or workflow vs conventional AI tools and existing solutions.
- **Technical Quality**: sound orchestration, appropriate use of supported CLI capabilities and expert agents, permissions and error handling, reliable execution for its stage.
- **AI NAS Fit & Validation**: meaningful use of UGREEN AI NAS capabilities (local data, storage, compute, privacy) rather than merely running on a NAS.
Round 1: pass/fail viability screen, then scored on the PDF + ZIP, proposed functionality, planned capability use and validation plan. Lack of NAS runtime evidence does not lower the Round 1 score. Tie-break order: User Value first.

## Prizes
Gold $20k, Silver $10k, Bronze $6k, 2 × Most Creative $5k (highest Innovation & Differentiation). Decided by Round 2 scores only.
